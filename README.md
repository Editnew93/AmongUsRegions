# AmongUsRegions
A Console App designed to help add custom regions for PC players.
<img width="1023" height="848" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/da1a278c-2de6-438e-9c89-dc095c7840b8" />

## Introduction

AmongUsRegions is a free console app to add or change regions in your Among Us.
Adding Modded Regions, Custom Impostor Servers...

Please DM me on discord (.editnew93) if you want to add any server here.

<img width="283" height="199" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6941eec5-1cde-403b-aef6-ca9c2c781a4a" /> 
<h6>This app basically prints a list of pinged and successful servers from our apps list.</h6>

## Use
Download the console app from [our releases ](https://github.com/Editnew93/AmongUsRegions/releases). Run it.

<img width="348" height="255" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/12b356d0-818a-4841-812e-782df017bdab" />

You will see this. Press enter to choose one. Up and down arrow keys to choose. After you choose one, go down to Next. Do not worry when you see this.

Its just pinging the servers incase they are online.

<img width="353" height="135" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b9cb149c-f1ee-4602-82e9-9d64c8ff7d38" />

After that, choose regions that you want to add to your among us.

<img width="561" height="348" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f07c63a8-1ede-4b88-b5c6-bc0f43b7fbc5" />

Then press "Make!"

> [!CAUTION]
> If you do NOT save any data while you are going to do this or you are the host, leave immediately and wait at the main menu or close among us.

<img width="453" height="119" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/177ffc16-2df3-4c65-b587-2a2ecf748965" />

You will see these things while making.

<img width="587" height="421" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c65933ed-4df1-458a-97bc-f4823b94768a" />

(Keep the target file private if you are on Microsoft or Xbox while ***streaming***.)

Launch among us. and there you go!

> [!TIP]
> Europe, Asia and North America is forced to be added. Theres no way to remove them since they are the official Among Us servers.
